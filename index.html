About the Book
Stop using AI like a calculator.
Start using it like a career architect.

The developer's role is undergoing its biggest transformation in a generation. AI capabilities are following what industry leaders call a "Moore's Law for intelligence" — doubling every few months — and analysts warn that 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs will be significantly impacted within 1–5 years. Engineers at leading AI companies are already shifting from writing code to editing, reviewing, and orchestrating AI-generated output.

The question is no longer whether AI will reshape your career — it's whether you'll be the one directing the change or reacting to it. The Augmented Developer bridges that gap. Across 26 chapters and six focused parts, this book walks you through integrating AI into every facet of your professional life: from planning architecture and writing tests to crafting resumes that pass ATS filters, acing system design interviews, and negotiating compensation packages worth tens of thousands more.

Every recommendation is evidence-based and paired with measurable outcomes. No hype, no hand-waving — just practical frameworks, real prompts, and actionable playbooks you can start using this week.
